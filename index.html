Gathering…
Files travel directly between the two devices, encrypted by WebRTC (DTLS) — this is the browser's standard single-layer transport encryption; Beam does not add a second, application-level encryption layer on top of it. Nothing is uploaded or stored — a relay, when needed, only forwards already-encrypted packets. Relay credentials are short-lived and fetched at runtime; no secret is stored in this page.
On iPhone: received files open in a preview — tap the Share icon to save to Photos or Files.
